# RealityFork at Kiroween 2026

## Enter Costume Contest

In the finished release, the haunted village teaches the decision-making workflow. The Keeper’s Desk frames a choice, the Whispering Well advances a timeline, and the Forking Mirror brings alternate timelines together. Signs and village details reflect what Mara chose. The chapter hands the visitor back to the planner to try a decision of their own.

Frankenstein is defensible because the product combines an authored 3D chapter, a branching decision graph, validated AI drafts, and a separate seeded simulation. Costume Contest gives the judges a clearer demonstration. The same interaction that gives the app its Halloween identity also explains how to compare choices and trade-offs.

## Show one trade-off

Open with the two futures for Mara’s books in the Forking Mirror. Then show how the visitor made the choices and transfer the same method to a real decision about a shop. End on requirements, steering, and a property test that helped enforce the behavior. Use [the storyboard](../submission/kiroween-2026/demo.md).
